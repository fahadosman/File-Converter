Premium payments are processed by Paddle.com as the merchant of record. Paddle handles tax, invoicing, and payment compliance. Your payment agreement is with Paddle for the transaction; Files Converter provides the software service.

2. What you are buying

Premium unlocks higher usage limits and related features as described on the site at the time of purchase. Unless stated otherwise, access is for the subscription or billing period shown at checkout.

3. Refund eligibility

You may request a refund where required by mandatory consumer law in your country, or where Paddle’s buyer terms allow a refund for the transaction. We do not guarantee refunds for change-of-mind after substantial use of premium features, except as Paddle or applicable law requires.

If a technical failure on our side prevents you from using premium after a successful payment, contact us with your transaction reference; we will work with you and Paddle to correct access or arrange a remedy.

4. How to request a refund

  1. Use the receipt or customer link from your Paddle purchase email, if available, to manage the order.
  2. Or contact us via Contact with the email used at checkout and your Paddle transaction ID.

Paddle may require verification before issuing a refund.

5. Chargebacks

If you initiate a chargeback, Paddle and our fraud-prevention processes may limit future checkouts. Prefer contacting us first so we can resolve billing issues quickly.

We may update this Refund Policy to reflect legal, processor, or product changes. The “Effective date” below identifies the current version.
