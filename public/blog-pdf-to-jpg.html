Tips for PDF to JPG Conversion

  • Choose Appropriate Resolution: Higher resolution for printing, lower for web use.
  • Consider File Size: Balance quality with file size requirements.

  • Use for Text-Heavy Pages: Best for pages with images or graphics.

PDF to JPG vs Other Formats

While JPG is popular, consider other options:

  • PNG: Better for images with transparency or text.
  • TIFF: Higher quality but larger file sizes.
  • WebP: Modern format with good compression.

FAQ: PDF to JPG Conversion

Can I convert specific pages only?

Yes, most converters allow you to select individual pages or page ranges.

Will text quality be affected?

Text may appear pixelated in JPG format. Consider keeping important documents as PDFs.

What's the best resolution for JPG conversion?

300 DPI for printing, 150-200 DPI for web use, depending on your needs.
