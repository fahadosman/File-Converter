Word to TXT Converter: Extract Plain Text for Fast Editing (2026)

Plain text is useful for automation, translation, documentation pipelines, and quick editing. Word-to-TXT conversion removes layout noise and keeps only content.

Use cases

  • Preparing copy for CMS migration.
  • Feeding content into scripts or LLM pipelines.
  • Lightweight drafts for collaboration tools.

Best workflow

  1. Convert DOCX to TXT.
  2. Review bullets, headings, and spacing.
  3. Re-add minimal structure if needed.
  4. Save UTF-8 for multilingual text.
