WEBP to PNG Converter: Preserve Transparency Correctly

For logos and overlays, PNG is often preferred because of broad editor support and stable transparency handling. This guide helps you convert WEBP to PNG without visual artifacts.

When PNG is better

  • Transparent logos for websites or slides.
  • Design handoff files for older tools.
  • Assets that need lossless editing.

Conversion checklist

  1. Verify alpha channel before exporting.
  2. Avoid repeated format switching cycles.
  3. Check edge halos on dark/light backgrounds.
