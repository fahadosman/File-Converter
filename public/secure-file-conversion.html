Secure File Conversion Guide

Not every “free converter” works the same way. This guide explains how Files Converter handles your files, what warning signs to avoid on other sites, and how to protect sensitive documents.

How Files Converter protects you

  • In-browser processing — Standard tools convert on your device; we do not upload your document to our servers for processing.
  • HTTPS — The website and APIs are served over encrypted connections.
  • No malware bundles — Downloads are your converted file only, not installers or browser extensions.
  • Transparent policies — Privacy, Security, and Terms describe real behavior, not fake certification logos.

Red flags on risky converter sites

Security researchers have flagged some converter sites for storing uploads too long, pushing malware, or impersonating brands. Watch for:

  • Domain names that look similar but are not filesconverter.org
  • Forced software installs before you get your file
  • Missing privacy or contact pages
  • Pop-ups claiming your computer is infected

Checklist before you convert

  • Confirm the URL is https://filesconverter.org
  • Classify the file: public, internal, or highly sensitive
  • For sensitive data, consider offline desktop tools if your policy requires it
  • Review output before sharing externally
